21:30 01 — The person behind them no design school.

I came out of a commerce degree and ended up designing products.

The short answer is that I kept making things until someone paid me for it. I've been doing visual and graphic design since 2022, and somewhere along the way the part that hooked me changed. These days it's the UX: who is using this, what state of mind they're in, and what they actually need from the screen in front of them.

I studied commerce, with marketing as my major. The design I learned on real clients with real deadlines. It left gaps I've had to close on purpose, and it means I've never designed something that didn't have to survive contact with a developer or a client.
